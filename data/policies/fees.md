# Account Fees (SYNTHETIC DEMO POLICY)
Monthly maintenance fee for Everyday Checking is $10. The fee is waived when the account receives direct deposits totaling $500 or more per statement cycle, or keeps a minimum daily balance of $1,500.
Paper statement fee is $2 per month. E-statements are free.
Out-of-network ATM withdrawals cost $3 per transaction. ABC Bank ATMs are free.
