# Personal Loans (SYNTHETIC DEMO POLICY)
Personal loans range from $2,000 to $40,000 with terms of 24 to 60 months.
Eligibility depends on credit history, income, and existing debt. Applicants need a verifiable income source.
Credit decisions must never consider race, color, religion, national origin, sex, marital status, age, or neighborhood (zip code) as a proxy for a protected class.
